## 4. Recommended Generation Settings & Negative Prompts

When running these in any modern text-to-image pipeline:

* **CFG Scale / Guidance**: `7.0` to `8.5` (keeps strict adherence to culinary accuracy)
* **Sampler**: Euler_a or DPM++ 2M Karras (30 to 40 steps)
* **Color Grade**: Warm ambient color temperature (3200K–3800K warm tungsten/candlelight)
* **Master Negative Prompt**:
  ```
  oversaturated, cartoonish, low quality, blurry, deformed meat, plastic rice, neon green, artificial lighting, text watermark, signature, stock watermark, duplicate items, bad framing, cut off
  ```
