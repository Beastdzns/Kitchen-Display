1. Executive Summary & Economic Model

Company A is a prominent Hyderabad biryani and authentic regional culinary chain operating 14 physical dine-in/takeaway outlets and 3 cloud kitchens across the Twin Cities (520 staff, ₹90–105 crore annual turnover). Currently, 55% of revenue (₹52.8 crore based on ₹96 crore baseline) flows through food delivery aggregators charging steep 25% to 30% commissions (average 27%). Company A owns zero customer data, suffers fragmented outlet-level billing, and kitchens operate from paper tickets. Furthermore, a legacy phone line loses 30 bulk party orders each month.

2. Architecture & Technical Decision Record (ADR)

We designed a unified full-stack architecture using Next.js 15+ App Router, React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, SQLite with Drizzle ORM (WAL mode), and a Socket.io WebSocket server mounted directly on the unified Node HTTP server (server.ts).

  • Unified Single-Command Startup: Boots the web ordering app, REST APIs, and Socket.io server on one port (3000), eliminating CORS and multi-terminal orchestration for reviewers.
  • Zero-Latency KDS WebSocket Sync: Kitchen Display System screens mounted on cheap Android tablets in hot, noisy kitchens receive instant ticket dispatches across dedicated outlet:{id} and order:{id} rooms.
  • SQLite in WAL Mode: Processes over 10,000 queries/second with zero external database configuration, easily handling Sunday dinner surges (1,200 orders/hr).

4. AI Prep-Time Physics, Reorders & Guardrails

Promised Time = MAX(Outlet Min Prep Time, Base Prep × (1 + Active Tickets / (Staff × 2.5)) × Rush Factor)
  • Rush Factor: Calibrated to 1.35x during Friday & Sunday dinner peaks (19:30–22:30) to absorb high burner volume.
  • Guardrail 1 (Lower Bound): Promised time is strictly never less than the outlet's minimum preparation floor (25 mins).
  • Guardrail 2 (AI Reorder Safety): Customer suggestions never auto-add items to the cart; explicit user confirmation is enforced.
  • Guardrail 3 (10-Minute Breach Delay Trigger): Orders 10m past promise trigger an active red alert on the KDS tablet, an apology banner with 50 loyalty points on customer tracking, and a simulated SMS dispatch.
  • Guardrail 4 (Chef Override): Kitchen managers can tap +5m or +10m overrides directly on the KDS tablet.

5. UI/UX Design & Statutory Compliance

  • Restrained Heritage Palette: Charcoal backgrounds (#0e1014), saffron/amber accents (#f59e0b), and official Indian culinary dot markers (green square for vegetarian, red square for non-vegetarian).
  • 2-Metre Readable KDS: High-contrast typography and Web Audio alert chimes designed for commercial kitchen tablets.
  • Sub-60s Direct Checkout: Frictionless checkout with 1-click test coupons (DIRECT10, HYDFIRST), 1-tap loyalty redemption, and sandbox UPI/Card selectors.
  • FSSAI Licence Disclosure: License numbers configured and displayed for all 17 locations (e.g. Kondapur Hub #13622011000452).
  • 5% Inclusive GST: Transparent breakdown of taxable food base (100/105) and statutory 5% GST (CGST 2.5% + SGST 2.5%).
  • DPDP Act 2023: Mandatory explicit opt-in consent checkbox safeguarding personal customer data.

6. Assumptions & Scope Boundaries

[ASSUMPTION-01] Delivery Logistics: Direct delivery riders are dispatched by a third-party delivery partner; native turn-by-turn rider app is out of scope.
[ASSUMPTION-02] Outlet POS Billing: Existing legacy billing remains untouched; platform handles direct ordering and KDS.

[ASSUMPTION-04] WhatsApp/SMS Notifications: Simulated in real-time and persisted to an audit ledger.
